Come inside

Hi, princess.

This isn’t a list of everything you are. That would never fit. It’s a handful of small things I’ve noticed, kept somewhere you can find them.

01/ 03

01 — Your Eyes

I won’t describe the color. You already know it. What stays with me is how much a single look can carry. It can be over before anyone else notices, and somehow it’s the part that lingers.

That’s one of them. I won’t explain it. You’ll know the look.

02 — Your Kindness

Kindness isn’t the same as being nice. Nice is easy and public. What I mean is quieter: the things done without waiting to be thanked, and what they say about someone’s character.

You’d call it nothing. I keep a very different tally.

03 — You Thought Only Three?

I said three because three fits on a page. The first two were only where I started. The third is just you, in the ordinary hours, not performing anything. The list doesn’t end, because there are too many small things to count.

You never have to become anyone else to be worth appreciating.

There is more to you than three reasons.

The Details That Make You, You.

Beauty isn’t only something people see. A lot of it is something they notice, slowly, in how someone is.

The way you look

Appearance is only what anyone sees first. It matters, and it’s the smaller part of the story. Purple suits you, by the way. It’s why this whole place leans that way.

Your eyes first, which is why they got a whole chapter. Then the smile. Then the pout, which I’m not supposed to admit I love.

The person behind it

Her look and her personality don’t feel like two separate things. The way you think, the way a plain conversation turns interesting once you’re in it, the way you say things in a way nobody else would. It all reads as one person.

You can be an airhead, and it’s charming. You can be sharp, and it’s fast. You can be jealous, and you’re terrible at hiding it. Same person every time, and every version is the one I’d pick.

The little things

More than a pretty face

If every photo of you vanished, almost nothing I’ve written here would change. You’re worth appreciating because of how you pay attention, how you treat people, and how you’re imperfect in a way that makes you easier to love. That was never the part that needed fixing.

There’s more to you than one page can hold.
